## Context

The situation that forced a choice: the constraint, the incident, or the number. Name the design that existed before.

## Decision

What was chosen, in one or two paragraphs.

## Why

The evidence for the choice. Incidents, measurements, and failure analyses go here with enough detail that a reader can check the reasoning.
